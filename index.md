## What Prosperity does

Prosperity tracks activity in your server. Every message a member sends earns a small amount of XP, on a cooldown you control, and members level up as that XP adds up. You decide which roles are granted at which levels, where level-up messages appear, and which channels or roles are left out of XP entirely. There is nothing to host and no dashboard to learn: everything is configured with slash commands.

<div class="home-card">

### Levelling that runs itself

Members gain 15 to 30 XP per message, throttled by a per-server cooldown. The level curve is fixed and predictable, up to level 1000.

[How levelling works](/guide/how-levelling-works)

</div>

<div class="home-card">

### Roles at every level

Grant a role automatically when a member reaches a level. Choose whether roles stack or swap as members climb.

[Level roles](/guide/levels-and-roles)

<div class="home-card">

### Tune it to your server

Set an XP multiplier and cooldown, pick a notifications channel, and exclude bot channels or muted roles from earning XP.

[Settings](/commands/settings)
